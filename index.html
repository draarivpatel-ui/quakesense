Arrivals and shaking vs distance

P and S arrivals, the alert instant, the blind span where S beats the alert, usable lead and soil-adjusted MMI, all for the configured source. Curves are modeled, not measured.

Preparedness comparator

Deterministic planning-model estimates at the chosen site distance and soil class, not engineering design values. EEW-triggered measures fail where the usable lead cannot cover their activation time; passive measures are always armed, and the hybrid package keeps its passive share without warning.
